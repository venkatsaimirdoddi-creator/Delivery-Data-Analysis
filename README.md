Technologies used:

## 📊 Project Overview
<br>

This project focuses on analyzing delivery data to understand delivery duration patterns and differences across cities.
<br>

The dataset was explored using Python and data analysis libraries to identify meaningful patterns and insights. The analysis includes examining the distribution of delivery duration, comparing delivery durations across different cities, calculating the average delivery duration for each city, and identifying the most common typecodes.
<br>

The goal of this project is to gain insights into delivery performance and understand the factors and patterns present in the delivery dataset.
<br>
## 🔍 Exploratory Data Analysis:
<br>

The dataset was explored to understand the patterns and distribution of delivery data.
<br>

The following exploratory analyses were performed:
<br>
- Analyzed the distribution of delivery duration.
<br>

- Compared delivery durations across different cities.
<br>

- Calculated the average delivery duration for each city.
<br>

- Identified the top 10 most common typecodes in the dataset.
<br>
 
- Used data visualization to better understand delivery patterns and variations.
<br>
These analyses helped identify differences in delivery performance between cities and revealed important patterns in the dataset.
<br>
## 📊 Key Insights

# 1. Distribution of Delivery Duration:
<br>

- Most deliveries are completed within a relatively short duration.
<br>

- The distribution is highly right-skewed.
<br>

- A small number of deliveries have extremely high delivery durations, indicating possible outliers.
<br>
# 2. Delivery Duration by Each City:
- Delivery durations vary across the three cities.
<br>

- Most deliveries in each city are completed within a lower duration range.
<br>

- Some extreme delivery duration values are present in all cities.
<br>
# 3. Average Delivery Duration by Each City:
<br>

- Chongqing has the highest average delivery duration at approximately 5.33 hours.
<br>

- Suzhou has an average delivery duration of approximately 2.40 hours.
<br>

- Shanghai has the lowest average delivery duration at approximately 1.77 hours.
<br>

- This shows that delivery performance differs significantly between cities.
<br>
# 4. Top 10 Most Common Typecodes:
<br>
- One typecode appears significantly more frequently than the others.
<br>
- The second most common typecode also has a large number of records.
<br>

- The remaining typecodes have comparatively lower frequencies.
<br>

- This indicates that a small number of typecodes dominate the dataset.
<br>
# Conclusion:
<br>

This project analyzed delivery data to understand delivery duration patterns, differences between cities, and the distribution of typecodes.
<br>

The analysis showed that most deliveries are completed within a relatively short duration, while a small number of deliveries have unusually long durations. Delivery performance also varies significantly between cities, with some cities having higher average delivery durations than others.
<br>

Additionally, the typecode analysis revealed that a small number of typecodes account for a large portion of the records.
<br>

Overall, this analysis provides useful insights into delivery patterns and highlights areas where delivery performance can be further investigated and improved.
